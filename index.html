The Socio-Economic Blueprint

🚀 Youth & Hyper-Employment Model

The youth are not just voters; they are India's primary economic engine. OPRDF replaces outdated rote-learning with a localized, industry-linked technical education matrix.

🏥 Universal Healthcare & Sovereign Sanitation

Health is a non-negotiable right, not a luxury. We pledge a total overhaul of community medical systems, implementing free, world-class diagnostic and tertiary healthcare grids across every district.

🌿 Anti-Pollution & Eco-Capitalism

An environment that kills its citizens cannot sustain economic growth. OPRDF introduces stringent industrial emission caps, massive urban forestry initiatives, and heavy investments in clean energy infrastructure.
